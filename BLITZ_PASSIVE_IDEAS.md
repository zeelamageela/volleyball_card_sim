# Blitz Passive Ideas (Brainstorm)

## Serve-Based
- **Aggressive Serving:** All serves have +2 value
- **Tempo Control:** Choose to serve or receive each rally (ignoring who won last point)

## Attack-Based
- **All-Out Attack:** Can commit 0 blockers after seeing opponent's block (draw those cards into hand)
- **High-Risk Setter:** Setter can attack from any zone using cards 7+
- **Fast Break:** After successful dig, can attack immediately with dig card (skip set phase)
- **Quick Transition:** After stuffing opponent, immediately attack with blind deck draw

## Block-Based
- **Pressure Read:** Before blocking, see opponent's highest card value
- **Counter Rhythm:** Choose to commit blockers before or after seeing attack (but if after, must blind draw)

## Deck Manipulation
- **All-In Offense:** Once per rally when attacking, discard any number of cards before drawing attack
- **Kill Cycle:** Score on attack kill → draw 1 extra card into hand
- **High-Card Filter:** At start of game, look at top 6 cards, arrange 3 top / 3 bottom
- **Aggressive Mulligan:** During refill, discard any cards 1-4 and redraw
- **Fast Tempo:** After scoring, shuffle discard + draw 1, keep or bottom-deck it
- **Momentum Swing:** Win 2+ points in a row → swap bottom of deck with hand card

## Notes
- Want: Aggressive, tempo-based, promotes attacking
- Avoid: Flat stat buffs
- Must work with both hand play and blind flips (for consistency with dummy teams)
